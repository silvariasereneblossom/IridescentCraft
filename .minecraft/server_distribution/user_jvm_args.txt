# JVM arguments for Forge's run.bat / run.sh. The pack's own launch scripts
# (iridescentserver.bat / .sh, start.sh) carry the same flags inline; all of
# them follow the pack launcher's (AIKAR_FLAGS in
# iridescent-launcher/icraft-core/src/config.rs).
#
# RAM: the server holds about 5-6 GB at idle, more with players. The heap
# starts at 4 GB (-Xms) and may grow to 10 GB (-Xmx), about 11-12 GB for the
# whole server. On a machine with less RAM to spare, lower -Xmx (6G is the
# lowest tested) and keep -Xms4G: a 2 GB start is outgrown at boot and ends
# higher. G means gigabytes, M megabytes.
-noverify
-Xmx10G
-Xms4G
# The heap can shrink back after a busy spell (at G1's next marking cycle).
-XX:MinHeapFreeRatio=10
-XX:MaxHeapFreeRatio=30
# Aikar's G1 flags. No AlwaysPreTouch: it made all of -Xms resident at start.
-XX:+UseG1GC
-XX:+ParallelRefProcEnabled
-XX:MaxGCPauseMillis=200
-XX:+UnlockExperimentalVMOptions
-XX:+DisableExplicitGC
-XX:G1NewSizePercent=30
-XX:G1MaxNewSizePercent=40
-XX:G1HeapRegionSize=8M
-XX:G1ReservePercent=20
-XX:G1HeapWastePercent=5
-XX:G1MixedGCCountTarget=4
-XX:InitiatingHeapOccupancyPercent=15
-XX:G1MixedGCLiveThresholdPercent=90
-XX:G1RSetUpdatingPauseTimePercent=5
-XX:SurvivorRatio=32
-XX:+PerfDisableSharedMem
-XX:MaxTenuringThreshold=1
-Dusing.aikars.flags=https://mcflags.emc.gs
-Daikars.new.flags=true
# On an OutOfMemoryError the heap dump is java_pid<pid>.hprof in this folder.
-XX:+HeapDumpOnOutOfMemoryError
