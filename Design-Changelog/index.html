    A high-level, dated summary of the major design changes in IridescentCraft, newest first. It is written for players: one headline per change and no implementation detail. The full development history (root causes, script-level notes) is in the project's private developer log.

    For where systems stand today, see the Master Design Document and the Home page status table.

    October 2026: Iron, coal, copper and quartz ore are half as common in new land (22 October)

    • Newly generated Overworld land holds half the iron, coal, copper and quartz ore it did. Each of these ores now tries fewer veins in a chunk, tuned so that a new chunk holds half the ore (measured over several hundred chunks). A vein is as big as before and sits at the same heights and in the same biomes, so a vein you find is as rich as ever; there are fewer of them. A new chunk holds about 58 quartz ore blocks instead of about 115. The raw iron and raw copper blocks inside iron and copper veins, and the coal blocks inside coal veins, are halved with them.
    • Large Ore Deposits' big iron, coal and copper lodes are half as frequent: about one lode in 2,000, 1,600 and 2,900 chunks instead of one in 1,000, 800 and 1,450. A lode is as big as before, and the flowers above it still mark it. The other lodes (gold, diamond, the modded metals) are unchanged.
    • Only new land changes. Chunks that are already generated keep the ore they have. When part of the world is regenerated, the regenerated land gets the new amounts as well.
    • Other ores are unchanged: gold, diamond, redstone, lapis, emerald, zinc, the Thermal and Mekanism ores, and everything in the Nether. With fewer iron and copper veins in the stone they turn up a little more often in new land (up to about a fifth more), because the veins that used to fill that stone are gone.

    October 2026: Quartz is a Tier 1 material (22 October)

    • Quartz is Tier 1. Quartz, its blocks (smooth, chiseled, pillar, bricks, stairs and slabs) and the quartz ores can be used and crafted from the start, and JEI shows them. Since April they were Tier 3, which also locked Create's rose quartz and with it the electron tube, so a Tier 1 player could not build a Deployer, a Mechanical Crafter, brass funnels and tunnels, smart chutes, or the other Create machines that take an electron tube. All of them can be built at Tier 1 now.
    • Quartz ore generates in the Overworld again, in stone and in deepslate, at every height. A freshly generated chunk holds about 120 quartz ore blocks, more under mountains and fewer under the sea. Only chunks generated from now on have it: land that is already explored has none, so look for it in new land. The Nether still opens at Tier 3.
    • Master masons sell quartz blocks and pillars again, 1 emerald each, and buy quartz from expert level as before. The calcite trade that replaced them on 19 October is gone. A mason keeps the trades it already offers, calcite included.
    • Occultism's miners can bring up nether quartz ore from the first miner spirit, as in Occultism itself. Since April only the Afrit and Marid could.

    October 2026: Mob drops and the last chests roll their extras once (19 October)

    • Endermen, bats, squid, zombies, drowned and spawners roll Forbidden & Arcanus's extras once. When Forbidden & Arcanus added its own drop (ender pearl fragments, bat wings, tentacles, zombie arms, spawner scrap), it ran every other loot rule a second time, as it did in chests until earlier the same day. An enderman killed in the End now drops a Codex Token III about one kill in twenty, the rate its loot rule gives (it was one in ten), and endermen anywhere drop half the RFTools Dimensions lost knowledge and dimlets they did (lost knowledge about one kill in eight instead of one in four). Forbidden & Arcanus's own drops are unchanged, and bats, squid, zombies, drowned and spawners drop what they did. This applies from the next server restart.
    • Abandoned mineshaft chests roll Forbidden & Arcanus's extras once too. They were the last chests that ran every loot rule a second time: a mineshaft chest now holds about half a Codex Token I instead of one, and half the artifacts, treasure fragments, backpacks and music sheets it did, matching the other structure chests. Forbidden & Arcanus's own items in them are unchanged. Valhelsia Structures' treasure jars got the same fix; their second roll added nothing, so a broken treasure jar gives what it did.

    October 2026: Summons, chest loot and the tester sync (19 October)

    • Dark Pact reaches every blow of an Iron's Spells summon. The Void Summoner's +15 % reached tamed animals and raised skeletons' arrows only; summoned zombies, vexes, polar bears and Iron's summoned blades struck without it. They now hit 15 % harder too, on top of the Summoner's spell power their blows already carried: about 27 % above a classless player's summon, where it was 10 %.
    • Chests roll Forbidden & Arcanus's extras once. In End City and bastion treasure, jungle temples, desert pyramids, underwater ruins, pillager outposts, dungeons and the three village smiths, Forbidden & Arcanus's additions ran every other loot rule a second time. Those chests now hold about half the Codex tokens they did (End City treasure about 2.2 Codex Token III a chest instead of 4.5, bastion treasure 1.5 instead of 3.3, an Overworld structure chest about 0.5 Codex Token I instead of 1), half the Tetra scrolls in dungeons and darts in jungle temples, and village smith chests about 6 iron ingots instead of 10. Village artifacts stay at about one chest in ten. This applies to chests opened from the next server restart.
    • Testers: the sync's unused second mod downloader is gone. Mods have always arrived through the sync's own downloader; the packwiz-installer path it also carried never ran on a tester's instance and is removed with its two jars. Once a launch has updated the sync, it deletes those leftover files from the instance.

    October 2026: The MekaSuit is a space suit (19 October)

    • A full MekaSuit counts as a space suit on every planet and orbit: you need no oxygen, and Ad Astra's heat and cold don't touch you, whether the suit is charged or not. Until now it gave no space protection at all, and a player in a full MekaSuit froze on the Moon and Mars.
    • Each planet's own dangers still need the planet enchantments. The cold of Mars, Mercury's night and Glacio, Mercury's sun and Venus's acid are not stopped by any suit; Thermal Regulation, Stellar Shield and Pressure Shell protect you from them.
    • Texts. The MekaSuit Mk2 is upgraded at a crafting table with Aethersteel, an Antimatter Pellet and Primordial Essence: the Codex and its lore no longer say it is forged at the Mythic Forge or needs Glacio materials. The Codex's enchanting page no longer says chests hold no enchanted books.

    October 2026: Villagers trade for convenience (19 October)

    • No villager sells an enchanted book any more. Librarians keep their other trades (paper, books, bookshelves, lanterns, glass, clocks, compasses, name tags). For enchants, use the enchanting table or the Relic Broker.
    • No villager or wandering trader sells diamonds, or diamond and netherite gear. Diamonds are a Tier 3 material. Until now expert and master smiths sold enchanted diamond armor, tools and weapons for emeralds, MCA's adventurers sold diamonds, and wandering traders sold a diamond for 3 emeralds and, as rare trades, diamond gear with Mending and very high enchant levels. The smith levels that sold diamond gear sell a convenience item instead: horse armor from the armorer (iron at expert, gold at master), an anvil from a master toolsmith, a shield from a master weaponsmith. Smiths still buy your diamonds for emeralds. MCA's adventurers no longer sell an enchanted golden apple, which tier-1 chests do not hold either.
    • Nothing a later tier holds back is for sale. Wandering traders sold a beacon for 25 emeralds and eyes of ender for 2, and master masons sold quartz blocks (beacons and quartz are Tier 3, eyes of ender Tier 4). Master masons sell calcite instead, 4 for an emerald.
    • Clerics sell XP bottles earlier: 6 emeralds a bottle at journeyman and 4 at expert, 8 bottles a restock each. The master's bottle for 3 emeralds is unchanged.
    • A villager keeps the trades it already offers. Only trades it gets from now on (a new villager, or one that levels up) follow these rules, so a librarian who already sells an enchanted book keeps selling it. This applies from the server restart after it lands.

    October 2026: Boss loot and the Codex's class pages (19 October)

    • The Ender Dragon's drops land once. One of Forbidden & Arcanus's dragon drops rolled every other drop rule a second time, so a kill gave two Dragon Hearts, two stacks of artifact pouches, double Rift shards and Iron's Spellbooks' dragon loot, and 1.1 Ultimate Reforging Tokens a kill instead of the designed 0.6. Each now drops once; Forbidden & Arcanus's own dragon drops are unchanged.
    • The Ancient Remnant drops artifact pouches and Awakenings like the other tier-4 bosses: two pouches a kill and about a 9 % chance at an Awakening.
    • The Baby Leviathan is off the Tier 3 boss rush. It only hatches from the Leviathan's egg, as a pet, so clearing 100 % of the rush meant hatching one and killing it. The rush now counts 44 bosses, and killing one pays no boss tokens.
    • The Codex's class and race pages say what the game does. The Berserker's +15 % is with axes (bows and spell books hit weaker); the Artificer has no ore bonus; the Paladin has no shield or sword bonus and heals 25 % more from potions and spells; a Void Summoner's Dark Pact reaches tamed animals and raised skeletons' arrows, while other Iron's summons strike with the Summoner's spell power. The Demi-God has four extra hearts and Speed II outside the Nether (cooked meat is not boosted), the Kirin runs at 2.5 times the base speed, the Ryu's healing is halved only while Regeneration is on, and the Fallen Angel has Haste I and glows. The enchantment page lists Primal Force, and Crowd Control counts nearby hostiles.

    October 2026: Glass cannons, the Mythic Forge and planet cold (19 October)

    • Glass cannons halve only their gear's health. The Ranger, Archmage, Void Summoner and Elemental get half the max health their armor, held items, curios, Vitality and HP affixes give, as the class pages say. Until now the game halved everything above a fixed base: a Demi-God Archmage with no gear had 19.8 health instead of 22.4, and Spice of Life, level-up, Health Boost and Ascension health were halved too, while a Faefolk or Fallen Angel kept all of its gear's health. Gear also lost 40 % of its health instead of half: an Archmage with a +4 chestplate has 17.6 health now, not 17.9.
    • The Mythic Forge stays in the crafting grid. It is the station for the five Mythic Catalysts; until now every catalyst craft used it up, so each catalyst also cost a new Forge.
    • Texts. The Engineering capstone quest no longer says a Mythic Catalyst V turns antimatter into gear: nothing applies a catalyst yet, and the MekaSuit Mk2 upgrade is where antimatter becomes gear. Thermal Regulation's text and the Codex say it covers the slow cold of Mars, Mercury's night and Glacio, not the freezing of the Moon and Mars without a full space suit (3 health a second, which only the suit stops). The Codex enchantment page describes Phalanx (6 % less damage a level while you block with a shield) and Last Stand (a killing hit leaves you at 1 health, once every 5 minutes) as they work. The design notes list the origins that start with less health: Arachnid and Phantom 14, Slimebodied 15, Witch of Ink 16, Feline 18.

    October 2026: Tetra materials' magic bonuses sized to Iron's Spellbooks (19 October)

    • Every magic bonus a Tetra material gives is +5 % a module, added up. A material's bonus applies to each module made of it. Until now most of them multiplied once per module and once per piece: an aethersteel set gave x3.8 spell power, an iridium set x39, a converted Ignitium set x9 fire power, and an onyx set with an onyx wand and book x30 spell power. Twenty-eight others (enderium, deorum, knightmetal, the diamond in 26 converted mod armour sets, and more) gave nothing at all, although their files listed bonuses.
    • A full set of one material now stays within what Iron's Spellbooks' own armour gives. A metal with a school (enderium, deorum, the boss metals...) gives that school +70 % for a full crafted set; a converted Fiery, Froststeel, Ironwood, Resonarium or Horizonite set +80 % (an Iron's school set gives about +68 % on its school). A gem set gives +20 % for each of its bonuses. Spell power for every school stays only on gems and paper, where a set holds few modules; mana regen, cooldown reduction and the resists are gone from metals and skins. Aethersteel, terrasteel and the Ender Guardian material give flat max mana instead (+10 a module, +140 a set). Themed materials: fire, ice, holy, lightning and blood give +5 % of their school a module (a full set +80 %); shadow, ender and nature give no magic bonus. Every material's numbers are on the Tetra materials page.
    • Vestment and runed armour give the same mage stats whatever cloth or skin they are made of. A piece gives vestment +50 mana and +5 % mana regen, runed +10 mana, +5 % spell power and +5 % cooldown reduction, and a set adds them up (vestment +200 mana and +20 % regen; runed +20 % spell power and cooldown). Until now they grew with the material and multiplied per piece: a leather runed set gave x2.4 spell power and cooldown, a leather vestment set +1,000 mana. A converted Iron's Spellbooks set (Pyromancer, Cryomancer) now gives x1.58 spell power instead of x1.98; the armour skins' own bonuses are unchanged.
    • Arcane cloth no longer carries Iron's Spellbooks' hidden cooldown bonus. Iron's Spellbooks defines a material at the same name, and its +15 % cooldown a module was added to ours: a full arcane-cloth set had x5.35 cooldown reduction. It keeps +5 max mana a module.
    • Knightmetal, knight, steel and cloggrum give +5 % of their own armour, added once, instead of multiplying per module: a converted Knightmetal set had 288 armour and has 140, a crafted one 186 and now 102. A sword of them no longer raises its holder's armour. Refined obsidian's knockback bonus is gone: its armour already reaches the knockback cap.
    • Modular wands and spell books give the same bonus whatever they are made of. A wand gives +5 % spell power, +15 % mana regen, +15 % cooldown reduction and +22.5 max mana; a book's modules +20 max mana and +5 % cooldown (an Ars book +5 % mana regen), besides the Iron's Spellbooks book it was made from. Until now they grew with the material and multiplied: a boss gem's wand gave x1.86 spell power, a wand and book of one gem x3.7, a gem set with both x3.8; now x1.25, x1.47 and x1.68. A gem's own bonus (+5 % a module) still applies.
    • A Tetra item's tooltip shows these bonuses as a number: "+0.2 Fire Spell Power" on a converted Fiery chestplate means +20 %. The Tetra workbench's Status bars show the percent. This all applies from the server restart after it lands.

    October 2026: In-game texts say what the game does (18 October)

    • Origins texts. The Revenant takes 20 % less damage in deep darkness (Resistance I), not 30 %. The Artificer's Resourceful is Speed I (+20 %) near a crafting table; the ore bonus and faster machines it promised were never built. The Ranger's +20 % ranged damage, the Berserker's Battle Trance (+5 % melee, +1 armor) and the Vanguard's Guardian's Presence (Weakness I on nearby mobs) read as they work, and the Paladin's text names its +25 % healing instead of a shield bonus it never had. The trinity mages' pools are +100 % Iron's mana; the Elemental's HP ties the Archmage's.
    • Enchantments and aptitudes. Primal Force tops out at +12 % a level (saturation stops at 20); Crowd Control counts the hostiles beyond the first. Arcane Efficiency and Materials Science give back a quarter of the levels while the menu is open; Brutal Slash adds up to +5 % against armored mobs.
    • Tetra armor and wands. The Runed schematics describe the Runed weave (they had the Vestment's text). Goggles add mana and spell power, the decorative trim adds armor, the wand cap has nothing for cast speed, and the wand's tier comes from honing. Some 25 small parts no longer promise effects they never had.
    • The End. The Dragon Summoning Crystal is used by holding it and sneaking at the fountain, and the texts now say so. The "End City" milestone is reached 1000 blocks out. A full-moon warning that never appeared is gone.
    • The Codex submit message names the lane you submitted to.
    • Quests. The Finale no longer says the Dragon unlocks Ad Astra (its planets open at Tier 4). The rune quests say runes make upgrade orbs, which the Arcane Anvil applies. The Mekanism ore quests give the real order, 2x to 5x, each step a machine in front of the last. Frostmaw's tip no longer suggests fire resistance (its breath freezes). The two diamond quests have their own names and say they complete when Tier 3 is granted, and two "provisional" notes are gone.

    October 2026: Unique weapons (18 October)

    • Every Simply Swords unique drops from a boss. Five tier-4 swords that no boss dropped, and that could not be crafted or found, now drop at 15 % a kill: Magiscythe and Magispear from Virtue, Wickpiercer from Lucifer, Harbinger from the Ender Golem, the Watching Warglaive from the Void Worm.
    • Frostmaw's Cyroheart Bow is a Tier 2 unique, like Frostmaw since it moved to Everbright. Its arrows deal +20 % instead of +10 %; the drop is unchanged.
    • Three uniques that never existed are off the lists. Twilight (Mowzie's Naga), Enchanted Estoc (Enchanter Merlin) and Dreadtide are names the installed Simply Swords does not make into items, so those two bosses never dropped anything for them. The pack has 42 unique swords and 34 bows.
    • The Ultimate Bosses have a tier in the Codex: the Storm (five Phantom Membranes) Tier 1, the Sorcerer (a Nether Star) Tier 3, both called inside the mod's towers on Overworld hills.

    October 2026: Magic bonuses count once (17 October)

    • Each magic bonus counts once on Iron's spells. Since the class system started working on 10 October, an Iron's spell got several bonuses twice. Mana Attunement (+5 % a tier) and the Magic aptitude skills Mana Spark, Mana Blaze and Mana Inferno sat on both spell power and magic damage, and a "magic damage sync" copied Arcane Supremacy (+50 %), Faefolk (+30 %), Elf (+5 %) and a Battlemage +15 % onto spell power, although Iron's spells already take magic damage. Mana Attunement and the three skills are now spell power, as their descriptions say, and the Origins bonuses are magic damage, as theirs say. The sync is gone. An Archmage's firebolt at tier 4 now does about 2.2 times what a classless caster's does (it was about 3.4 times). Magic damage does not reach Ars Nouveau spells; it never did.
    • The Archmage's texts on the Origins screen say what the class does. Mana Attunement is +5 % spell power for each passive tier above the first (+15 % at Tier 4); there is no spell-kill mana and no standing-still bonus, and the melee malus is Frail Melee's -50 %. The mana pool is 2.5 times the baseline (Mana Reservoir's +150 %: 250 instead of 100).
    • The mage classes' mana pools are their own powers' again. Since 10 October a script multiplied them by 1.5 on top of each class's pool power: the Archmage had 375 mana, the Battlemage and the Void Summoner 285. Now the Archmage has 250 (2.5x), the Battlemage and the Void Summoner 190 (1.9x), as their Origins texts say; the Elemental, the Blessed and the Corrupted keep 200 (2x). The Codex said 1.5x for all three; it says the real numbers now.
    • The Battlemage's texts say what it does. +10 % melee (the description said 15 %) and +15 % on magic hits; Mana Shield is Resistance I at all times, Resistance II for a Faefolk (there is no 15 % chance to negate a hit, and gear does not raise it); melee kills restore 15 mana.
    • The Void Summoner's Dark Pact reaches Iron's Spells summons. Its +15 % damage worked for tamed animals only; summoned zombies, skeletons, vexes and the rest now deal +15 % too, as the power always said. Soul Tether's text says what it does (every death within 16 blocks heals you 5 % of the creature's max health, up to 1 heart, and gives 1 XP), and so does Shadow Cloak's (+10 % melee damage in darkness, no penalty in light).

    October 2026: Data fixes (17 October)

    • "Random Spell Scroll" rewards give a scroll. Five Practical Magic rewards named a loot table that did not exist and gave nothing; each gives one Iron's Spellbooks scroll now, bound to a random tier-1 spell the moment it lands in your inventory (as the Relic Broker's scrolls are).
    • Iron Tools gives 3 iron ingots instead of an item that does not exist.
    • The Boss Compass leads to the Thunker. Its row pointed at the Chthonic Cathedral, where no Thunker stands; it points at the Chthonic Dungeon now, whose halls and rooms hold Thunkers (they also roam every Nether biome).
    • The Ad Astra planets count for the class passives' tier, like their orbits, the End and the Aether: landing on a planet raises the passive tier to 4 (Samurai Strength IV, Archmage +15 % spell power), as entering an orbit already did.

    October 2026: The Twilight Forest portal lights again (17 October)

    • The Twilight Forest portal can be lit again. Throw a Codex Token I (the Tier 1 Codex token) into a pool of water ringed by grass with flowers on it; a diamond, the mod's own key, works too. Since April the only key had been a leftover token that nothing gave, so no survival player could open the portal. Anyone can light it, but stepping through still needs Tier 2. This applies from the next server restart.
    • Lucifer's Token is retired from the design. It was to be a second combat door to Tier 4 (bank your way up, then kill Lucifer); it was never built. The combat road to Tier 4 is the full Tier 3 boss rush, Lucifer included. The Codex and the quests Capstone: Lucifer, Boss-Rush III and Ascension III say so now.
    • The Ad Astra planets do not wait for the Ender Dragon. They and their orbits are Tier 4 dimensions: the Tier 4 stage and a rocket take you there. The Codex said the dragon kill opens them; it never did, and it will not.
    • Frostmaw's Tetra parts are Tier 2 parts. The book covers, pauldrons and wand parts made of the Frostmaw crystal kept their Tier 1 numbers when Frostmaw became a Tier 2 boss; they now match its armour parts (about a fifth stronger). This applies from the next server restart.

    October 2026: Wiki corrections (16 October)

    • Ultris's Ultra Wither cannot be summoned in this pack. The appendix listed it as a summonable boss. Ultris builds its bosses from vanilla mobs, and every Wither here is Progressive Bosses' own, so the Ominous Pearl never finds a Wither to transform; the stronger Wither is the Ultimate Wither. The other Ultris bosses exist but are on no boss list: no kill tokens, no boss-rush credit.
    • Class passives, as they work. Samurai's Vorpal goes up to Strength IV (the page said V); the Void Summoner's Soul Tether gives +1 XP and heals 5% of the mob's max health (up to 1 heart) for each mob dying within 16 blocks (the page said +10% XP). Both tier-scaled passives use the highest tier of dimension you have entered.
    • Boss rush rosters: 9 / 18 / 45. Three pages still gave 10 / 18 / 43 after Merlin and Circe became Tier 3 bosses.

    October 2026: The Chthonic Cathedral's bosses are Tier 3

    • The Chthonic Cathedral's bosses are Tier 3 bosses. Enchanter Merlin (was Tier 1), Sorceress Circe (was Tier 4) and Archmage Gundalf (was Tier 2) are all met at the Nether's Chthonic Cathedral: Occult Fabric on its Offering Pedestal calls Merlin and Circe together, and Gundalf appears when Circe falls. Merlin and Circe are on the Tier 3 boss rush now (Tier 1 → 2 needs 8 of 9 bosses, Tier 3 → 4 all 45). Their kill tokens, the Magic Estoc (Merlin), the Astral Bound (Circe) and Circe's Tetra material follow them to Tier 3.
    • Occult Fabric in the Nether. The chance of Occult Fabric in a chest moved from the Overworld's Infested Laboratory to the Chthonic Dungeon, where the Gatmancers live (12 %).

    October 2026: The Cardinal Sins arenas generate again

    • The Cardinal Sins have arenas again. Since the end of May no Cardinal Sins arena generated anywhere: the mod builds its arenas only in the Overworld, and the pack had moved them to the Undergarden and the Nether, so the shrine chain could not start. Now the seven sins and Drakara have their arenas in the Abyss, and Lucifer's arena is in the Nether. The Boss Compass finds every one of them. They stay Tier 3 bosses, and Lucifer stays the Tier 3 capstone.
    • The shrine chain. Every shrine takes the previous sin's Soul Orb and works once: Sloth → Gluttony → Lust → Greed → Envy → Wrath → Pride → Lucifer's Atrocity Shrine. Sloth's Malevolichite Shrine takes a Soulweaver Orb (8 Malevolichite around an Eye of Ender; the ore sits in the floating pillars of the Overworld's Forgotten Forest). Every other orb is an Eye of Ender and the Essence the previous sin drops, at a smithing table with no template.
    • The Boss Compass points to the right Terramity places. Enchanter Merlin and Sorceress Circe are summoned together with Occult Fabric at the Offering Pedestal of the Nether's Chthonic Cathedral, Archmage Gundalf appears when Circe falls, and Gatmancers live in the Chthonic Dungeon. The compass pointed at the Fairy Fountain, the Mausoleum, the Trial Spire and the Infested Laboratory, where none of them is found.

    October 2026: Aether follow-ups

    • Neptune armour converts like zanite armour. At the Tetra workbench it becomes iron modular armour; it keeps its look, its armour bonus and its full-set bonus (water breathing and dolphin's grace). The old conversion multiplied spell power by about two per piece, so a full set multiplied it by eighteen. The Neptune Tetra material is gone.
    • Converted Forbidden Arcanus armour can be repaired. Mortem pieces with cloth, tyr pieces with golden dragon scales, draco arcanus pieces with dragon scales (the repair items named before did not exist).

      • Blessed (Hierophant): holy, nature, evocation. The sustain and support crusader: hardier, with the best healing, and strong against undead and the Corrupted.
      • Corrupted (Hollow): blood, ender, eldritch. The attrition drain-mage: it sustains itself through lifesteal and resists the elements, but is vulnerable to holy.
    • The three form a rock-paper-scissors triangle through their resistances (Blessed beats Corrupted beats Elemental beats Blessed). Each beats the Archmage within its three schools and falls behind across the other six. Each starts with a themed spell kit. (This is the first phase: their signature active abilities and the offensive side of the triangle arrive in a follow-up.)

    June 2026: Glass-cannon armor maluses now scale instead of wiping robe builds

    • T1 damage softener trimmed from -30% to -15%. The early Overworld is meant to be punishing for the low-HP mage origin stacks (glass cannons earn their late-game ceiling by being fragile early). The +30% damage bump now carries that intent, so the softener is cut to a thin margin against one-shots instead of a comfort cushion.
    • Incoming damage raised +30% at every tier. A new damage-only setting in the difficulty mod makes mobs hit 30% harder across all four tiers (and at every point on the time curve); their health, armor, and speed are unchanged. This raises the threat in advance, to match the class and race power that the progression fix below restored.
    • Class/race progressions apply now. A command-syntax mismatch had silently disabled most attribute-based class and race bonuses since they were written (Witch of Ink defense ladder, Berserker Trance, Orc Bloodlust, Wanderer bonuses, Slimebodied DR, the whole Artificial Construct ladder, and the caster mana-regen/cooldown sync). Each now runs on a working mechanism. Damage-side effects were never affected.
    • Workbench labels completed. The 407 missing translation entries are filled. Honing schematics now show themed names (Flow, Potency, Bulwark, ...) with tier numerals instead of raw IDs, and wand parts and material categories have proper labels.
    • Carrot/potato harvest yield fixed. Right-click (hoe) harvesting took the crop's own replant cost out of a reduced drop pool, netting zero. The drops are retuned so every harvest method nets the designed yield.
    • 166 dead workbench material choices now work. Newer materials (boss metals, Aether alloys, wools, fibres, hides) could be selected in reforged armor, wand and spellbook schematics but silently did nothing. They now have real stats (scaled from material grade) and repair recipes. The numbers are a conservative first pass; expect tuning.
    • Armor sets now count every piece. A modifier-identity collision in the modular-armor pipeline meant every equipped reforged piece silently replaced the previous one's armor contribution, so a full set gave roughly one piece's worth (tooltips, which render per item, always looked right). Each slot now counts separately, and equipped armor matches your tooltip sum before percentages.
    • Class/race armor penalties converted from flat to percentage. The Archmage's armor malus was a flat -4 armor / -2 toughness, written when a typical kit carried ~20 vanilla armor (about -20%). Against today's Tetra robe builds (4-8 armor total), the same flat number erased the entire armor value; that was the "my robes read 0.5 armor" report. The Archmage is now -20% armor & toughness, the Ranger -15% armor, and the Elf race's frailty -10% toughness. Each applies to your final armor, so the penalty scales with what you wear and can never zero a build. The Codex class pages are updated to match.
    • Glass-cannon equipment-HP halving now halves. A feedback loop in the penalty calculation meant Ranger, Archmage and Void Summoner equipment HP was reduced by only about a third instead of the designed half, and the penalty could stay after the gear was removed. Both are fixed, and Void Summoners are now measured against their correct base HP.
    • New diagnostic: /icraft armormods. Any player can list every live modifier on their armor and toughness (including the ones items and powers apply invisibly), which helps report armor problems precisely.

    June 2026: Loot-leak audit, dead-pack revival, and sync hardening

    • Stray loot fixed at the source. Three separate mechanisms were leaking high-tier items onto things that shouldn't carry them. Passive animals no longer drop boss-fragment cores, hostile-mob projectile and beam entities are no longer equipped with gear or scaled, and script-equipped mob gear no longer drops on death. The over-broad death-loot rule was replaced with a handler limited to monsters.
    • Two dead datapacks revived. The stone-tag fixer and the infinite-ham blocker were silently rejected because of a packaging error (a missing or misplaced pack manifest). Both load and do their jobs again.
    • Affix and gem repairs. Several Apotheosis status-effect affixes had a malformed value shape and never loaded. Two custom gems (Guardian, Intelligent) were fully disabled by a duplicate-slot conflict. The magic-weapon affix pool came out empty because a shadowing override dropped the relevant weapon types. All of these are repaired.
    • Broken tag and ID cleanup. A sweep of armor, entity and biome tags and a handful of loot and recipe scripts fixed dozens of references to renamed, mistyped, or uninstalled-mod items, so they no longer cause errors at load.
    • Script error fixes. The boss-bonfire tick error, several Create/Industrial-Foregoing recipe failures, and a non-existent loot id were corrected.
    • PacketFixer removed, then restored the same day. It looked redundant alongside Connectivity, but its string-length patch turned out to be needed: without it, large server payloads broke client logins against a vanilla 32k string cap. It is back in the pack, and the harmless overlap warnings with Connectivity return with it.
    • Ash blacklist. Common passive mobs are now excluded from a Supplementaries ash interaction through a tag override (the config flag for it did nothing).
    • Stranded-item janitor. A periodic cleanup removes stray marker items left behind by the boss-wave randomizer.
    • Sync failures are visible. After a failed update the launcher no longer starts a stale pack without saying so: an in-game warning appears if the pack didn't sync, and a new-machine setup guide documents the correct install layout.
    • Scorching (formerly Ignition) affix reworked. It was accidentally giving the target fire resistance. It now marks struck enemies with a timed fire vulnerability that amplifies all fire damage they take, scaling with affix rarity.
    • MekaSuit Mk2 rebuilt. The Mk2 is now an in-place upgrade of the real MekaSuit: installed modules, stored energy, enchantments, and affixes all survive the craft, and the upgraded suit has bonus base armor on top of full module support. (The old version was a non-functional placeholder that destroyed installed modules. If you crafted one, ask an admin for a replacement.)
    • Mekanism rebalance (engineering stack, ERA 4). Fusion now generates 1.5x and fission 1.25x energy per fuel, so big reactors pay off against the pack's 2.5x machine power costs. Radiation is disabled (it could semi-permanently ruin a base). An overdamaged fission reactor now shuts itself down until it cools instead of melting down, which is still dangerous but always recoverable. Fusion, antimatter/SPS, the Digital Miner, and ultimate-tier facilities are now Tier 4; intermediate materials stay open. The 5x ore chain is untouched, as the reward for reaching Tier 3 and building the infrastructure.
    • Antimatter is now worth making. The top Mythic Forge catalysts (IV and V) each require an Antimatter Pellet, the MekaSuit Mk2 upgrade consumes one per piece, and pellets can be exchanged for Codex progression tokens, which makes the SPS the endgame power sink it was always meant to be.
    • Biofuel rework. All crops now crush into roughly a quarter of the biofuel they used to, so meaningful ethylene power needs automated farming. (Bio-generator burn values and the ethylene chain itself are unchanged.)
    • Worldgen rebalance (lands with the world reset). The world gets flatter and friendlier: noticeably more plains, lush deserts and volcanic biomes retired (scrubland and wastes remain), mountains about a quarter lower and smoother overall, and Cherry River Valley trimmed very slightly (still common). Towers of the Wild appear more often, Apotheosis tome towers slightly less often. On the tome-tower loot: the long-standing "chests under-fill" issue was traced to a book-stripping bug that had already been removed, and the arcane-essence and scroll additions have been live since they shipped, so a fresh world shows it all working.
    • Tier-gating regression fixed (fresh-world impact). A legacy gating script re-enabled in early June brought back whole-mod locks (Thermal, Ars Nouveau, Occultism, Forbidden & Arcanus) on top of the current per-item design. Veterans never noticed (they were already high-tier), but fresh-world players hit it immediately: starter spellbooks and Thermal seeds could not be picked up at Tier 1. The legacy script set is retired again, and gating follows the curated per-item lists. (Server operators: the stale-file cleanup is required for this to take effect.)
    • Pigs no longer drop boss loot. The long-running mystery of boss weapons, reforging tokens, waystone cores, and runes appearing on ordinary pigs is traced and fixed. The entity registry silently substitutes a pig for any misspelled or wrong-mod boss id in the loot system, and eight such ids had accumulated. All are corrected or removed, and a boot-time validator now reports any future unresolvable id loudly instead of letting it leak. (The Gaia Guardian's drops were never affected: its unusual internal name is correct.)
    • XP collection fixed (was gamebreaking). Since the Linear Experience mod shipped, XP and levels were accruing on the server, but the client bar never updated (the mod's interception bypassed the only field the game watches to re-send XP to your screen). Picking up orbs now visibly fills the bar and levels again.
    • The spawn village is now a safe haven. Hostiles can no longer spawn within the spawn village, and any that wander in are silently removed (with no drops and no XP, so the zone can't be farmed). In practice, raids cannot run inside the protected radius.
    • Waystone Cores re-specced. They are now a consistent uncommon (20%) drop across all twelve bosses, instead of either guaranteed or a coin flip.
    • Meat drops doubled. All base meat from the standard animals (pig, cow, mooshroom, chicken, sheep, rabbit, cod, salmon, hoglin) now drops at twice the base rate, raw and cooked, with Looting stacking on top. Food and cooking are central to the pack, and the economy now reflects that.
    • Last pig-loot straggler fixed. One more misspelled mob id (an ant "queen", which is a caste, not an entity) was feeding Nature Runes to pigs. The drop moved to the real ant at a much lower rate.
    • Stone is mineable again (critical fix). A mod was overwriting the "breakable by pickaxe" block list and wiping plain stone out of it, which left stone practically unbreakable. The previous datapack patch for this had stopped applying without any sign. The fix now sits at a layer that always wins regardless of mod load order, so it can't silently break again.
    • Cherry River Valley gets its rivers back. The valley biome's climate claim was unintentionally swallowing the band where the game carves rivers, so the "river valley" generated without rivers. The claim now splits around the river band (rivers flow through valleys again, and through mountain valleys too), and the valley's range extends slightly toward the coast, so more of them border lakes and seas. This applies to newly generated terrain and lands fully with the world reset.
    • Spellbook stat tooltips cleaned up. Modular spell books showed their magic stats twice: once correctly as percentages and once as redundant raw decimals (e.g. +0.05). The decimal lines are gone, and stats now show only as percentages.
    • Spellbook page materials fixed. Applying Deathskin (and other animal-hide materials) to a spell book's pages did nothing, because the pages slot was missing those material options. All four hide materials now apply correctly.
    • Three magic enchants now work. Spell Echo, Mana Siphon, and Resonance could be obtained but did nothing. Resonance now extends your beneficial potion durations (+50% per level) while you hold the book; Mana Siphon returns a slice of your spell damage as mana (capped so it can't be farmed); Spell Echo gives each cast a chance to cost no mana, on a short cooldown. Their tooltips now describe the real effect.
    • Spellbooks removed from village chests. Copper and novice spellbooks appeared in roughly one of eight village chests (several per village), which trivialized a mage start that the class starter kits already provide. Village chests still carry ink and source gems at unchanged rates; spellbooks now come from your class kit, exploration loot, and progression.
    • Priest tower in every village (and always at spawn). The Iron's Spells priest tower (inscription table, priest villager) used to be roughly a coin flip per village. Its weight is now raised across all village styles (~85%+ of villages, still at most one each), and the starting village is guaranteed one: if the spawn village didn't roll a tower naturally, one is placed at the village edge on first load. Existing worlds get one on their next boot.
    • Lovely Pieces curation pass. The accessory mod is now fully loot-driven: all crafting recipes are removed, and every item is tier-scoped into exploration loot (T1 dungeon chests, T2 progression-dimension chests, T3 nether-tier dimensions plus ancient cities and bastions, Gambler set pieces hunted across T3). The six legendary accessories are exclusive 10% drops on tier-matched bosses, with two hosts each: the Blasphemous Contract comes from the Lich or the Sun Spirit, the Dragon Heart from the Hydra or the Eye of the Storm, and so on. Our fork also cuts the FPS-scaled damage eye (non-deterministic and hardware-biased), the heartrate HUD, and the floating damage-number particles. All drop rates are provisional.
    • Iridescent Lovely Pieces (new custom mod, fork intake). A charm-focused accessory mod joins the pack as a managed fork: ~60 new curios (15 boots, rings, amulets, quivers, gadgets, a dark "legendary" tier with real tradeoffs) plus the Gambler set with its chip-gambling crit system. Upstream's game-breaking bug (a damage attribute that silently multiplied all unarmed and melee player damage to zero) is fixed in our build, the legendary auto-grant is off, and the floating damage-number particles it ships can be switched off in the config. Items are currently craftable; tier-gating and slot-row curation are the next pass. Because curios are now affixable, these accessories join the affix lane automatically.
    • Curios become affixable and socketable (curio affix lane). Two new mods land together. Apothic Curios bridges Apotheosis onto curios: every curio slot (rings, necklaces, charms, belts, backs, bracelets, spellstones, spellbooks, and more) can now roll affixes and gem sockets. Caster Curios Bonus adds 12 build-around caster accessories (cooldown-from-spell-power, mana overflow casting, lifesteal sharing, a temporary free-casting overdrive, and more), found in structure loot such as ancient cities and bastions. A 14-affix curio pool ships with it (offense on rings/bracelets/hands, defense on necklaces/bodies/backs/heads, utility on charms/belts/feet, caster stats on spellbooks), and all 14 custom gems gained a curio bonus matching their school or martial identity. Curios found in chests can roll pre-affixed from Tier 1, so players meet the system early. All numbers are provisional until a feel pass.
    • Mage curios join the village pool. Six caster-oriented curios (mana, cooldown, and cast-time rings, a concentration amulet, a mana-regen amulet, and a spell-discount ring) now roll in village chests alongside the martial set. The total village curio supply is unchanged: the pool is redistributed, and mage items are now about a third of artifact rolls.
    • Engineering questline. A new 14-quest Heracles line walks through the whole engineering lane: Create ore-doubling at Tier 1, Thermal infrastructure at Tier 2, the Mekanism 5x chain at Tier 3, and fusion and your first antimatter at Tier 4. It comes with Codex token rewards and a matching "Engineering Lane" Codex chapter. Also fixed: the MekaSuit Mk2 recipes referenced a nonexistent ingot id and were silently uncraftable.

    • Modded metals as Tetra materials: dozens of modded ores and special materials (Twilight Forest, Blue Skies, Undergarden, Forbidden & Arcanus, Abyss, Botania cloths, and more) were wired into the Tetra crafting system, with a consistent hammer-tier ladder.
    • Unified mana pool: Ars Nouveau and Iron's Spells & Spellbooks now share one mana pool, with gem buffs and elemental Apotheosis gems feeding school-specific spell power.
    • Crit system unified: all critical-hit sources were converted to a single additive number, with Vorpal reworked and a dedicated magic-weapon enchant set.
    • Bespoke difficulty engine: a custom time-and-dimension-based scaling mod (iridescent_difficulty) replaced ScalingMobs, Improved Mobs, and Azukaar's Fair Difficulty.
    • Magic progression: Dan's Magic and Simple Staves wands were integrated as a Tier 1 to 4 mage progression and folded into the modular wand system, and drop-wand tiers were added.
    • Origin progression rework: capstone abilities and in-game status commands for progression origins (Witch of Ink, Artificial Construct).
    • Armor weight system + Battlemage rework: explicit light/medium/heavy armor tagging, with a toughness trade-off for light armor.
    • Loot rebalances: per-structure themed loot pools for the major structures, a village food and seed rebalance, a Celestial Artifacts re-audit, and several over-aggressive mod-loot strips.
    • Iridescent Aptitudes: the JustLeveling fork expanded with a five-tier skill-node pass across all eight aptitudes.

    March 2026: Foundations (progression, dimensions, and systems)
